Who is Neil Denby?

Born in Taumarunui in 1951, Neil Denby was the youngest of four children. His family moved through Eltham, Rotorua and Carterton, and he attended Kuranui College before choosing service in the Royal New Zealand Air Force.

Neil trained as an Avionics Technician and served for 24 years in the RNZAF, primarily at RNZAF Base Auckland, including three years as an instructor at Wigram. After leaving the Air Force in 1991, he moved into the security industry, working across domestic, corporate and government security environments throughout New Zealand.

Neil and his wife later moved to Matamata and built a home that allowed them to care for his mother-in-law while maintaining her own living space. Family, service and practical responsibility are recurring themes in his story.

“My duty is to those who will elect me as their representative.”

Why am I here?

Neil says he became dissatisfied with the available political choices and was drawn to the idea of a network of independent candidates. He is standing on the principle that an electorate representative should listen first, consult locally and remain free from party whips.

His stated call to action is ONE TICK INDEPENDENT.
